What is stored, and where

Your vault stays on this machine. Knowlu's servers hold your account, the judgments they make for you, and what you correct; they never hold the text of your notes, and nothing here is ever sold or shared.

On your machine

  • Your vault — the folder you chose. Plain Markdown files with a short header, one per task, plus a record of every change under state\journal\. You can read all of it in any text editor.
  • App data — %LOCALAPPDATA%\knowlu\: which profiles exist, your settings, when you last looked, and the last sixty run logs.
  • Coursework logins — Windows Credential Manager, under knowlu/<profile>/<source>. Never in the vault, never in a backup, never in a log.
  • Your backup folder, if you set one — a mirror of the vault and one dated snapshot a day, in a folder you chose and control.

What leaves the machine

  • Requests to the sources you connected: your LMS calendar feed, your coursework sites, and any campus event feeds you turned on.
  • One request a day to check whether a new version of Knowlu exists. It carries the version you are running and nothing else.

That is the whole list. There is no analytics, no crash reporting and no account — and so there is no switch to turn any of it off.

Copy diagnostics in the tray menu puts the version, the last three runs, the last error and your slot times on the clipboard. It never includes the text of a note, and nothing is sent until you paste it somewhere yourself.
