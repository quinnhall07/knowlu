What Knowlu is, and what it is not

Knowlu is a Windows app and a service behind it, and what it does is rank work you already have. It reads your school's calendar feed, the coursework sites you connect and — if you turn it on — your own inbox, and it answers one question every morning: what should you work on today, and in what order. It keeps what it finds as plain files on your machine and puts the day in front of you.

It is not a tutor, a grader, or a way of doing the work. Knowlu reads; it does not submit anything to your school for you. It never posts, never answers a question on your behalf, and never sends anything to an instructor. It can be wrong about what matters most today — it is ranking, not prophecy — and you are the one who decides what you actually do.

The summer pause

How to cancel

Every billing email carries the same way out: Stripe's receipts link straight to that portal, and our own emails say how to reach it — or you can reply to any of them and we will cancel it for you. You never have to have the app in front of you to get out. Cancelling stops the next charge and leaves you with what you have already paid for until that period ends.

Availability, and what does not work offline

We do not promise that the service is up. It is one small business's cloud, and it will occasionally be down; when it is, we fix it and say so.

The part of Knowlu that matters most in the morning does not need us: the ranking is deterministic and runs on your machine, so today's page still appears on a train with no signal. What needs the internet is what only the cloud can do — the judgments, the calendar and email fetches, sync, and the subscription check. If we cannot be reached, Knowlu keeps working on the last answer it had for three days, and after that it keeps ranking and names the steps it is skipping instead of pretending they ran.
