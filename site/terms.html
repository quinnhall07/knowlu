What Knowlu is, and what it is not

Knowlu is a desktop app and a service behind it, and it answers one question: what's next. It reads your school's calendar feed, the coursework sites you connect, your grades on Blackboard and, at schools Knowlu supports, your class schedule; your calendar; and, if you turn it on, your email, through forwarding to a private Knowlu address or through Gmail's own permission. From those it ranks the work you already have and puts the day in front of you. The ranking runs on your computer. The judgments it ranks with — how long something will take, how much it matters — are made by a language model in our cloud. What it finds is kept as plain files on your computer and, unless you choose This computer only, in your account.

It is not a tutor, a grader, or a way of doing the work. Knowlu never submits, answers or completes anything for you, never posts to a course site, and never sends anything to an instructor or to anyone else. What it does change in your accounts is listed, in full, under What you allow Knowlu to do.

Knowlu is not the official record

Knowlu copies due dates, grades and class times from your school's systems, and it estimates how long work will take and how much it matters. Knowlu will sometimes be wrong. A copy can lag or be wrong, a site can change without warning, a sign-in can expire, and an estimate is only a guess. Your course site, your syllabus and your instructor are the official record. Check a due date with them before you rely on it, and especially before you skip something because Knowlu did not show it. You decide what you do; Knowlu is a tool for planning it.
