Why I’m building Knowlu.

I’m a student. My week lives in Canvas, zyBooks, VHL, my school email and a calendar, and none of them talk to each other. The date that matters most is usually on page three of a syllabus.

So I started building Knowlu for myself. It reads all of those places and gives me one answer: what to do next. Anything it works out on its own waits for your yes. Your school passwords stay on your computer. And it never does your homework.

If that sounds like your week too, join the pilot and help me shape it.

Quinn

Questions.

When does it launch?

For spring semester 2027, on Windows and Mac. Pilot students get it first.

What does Knowlu read?

Canvas and other course calendars, zyBooks, VHL, your school email or Gmail, Google and Outlook calendars, syllabi you upload, campus events, and your registrar where your school offers it.

Does it do my homework?

No. Knowlu plans your time and explains why. It never does the work and never writes anything you send to someone else.

Where do my school passwords go?

Nowhere. They stay on your computer, and Knowlu signs in to your portals from there. What it reads, like assignments and due dates, goes to Knowlu’s servers to plan your day.

Pricing will be announced before launch. 30-day money-back guarantee on the yearly plan. Cancel anytime.
