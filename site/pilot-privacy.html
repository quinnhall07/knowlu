Pilot privacy, in plain words

Knowlu reads your school accounts so it can tell you what's next. Here is what that means for your data. The full privacy policy is what counts; this page is the short version.

Knowlu never sees your school password.

You sign in to your school on its own page. Logins for coursework sites like zyBooks are kept in your computer's own password storage, and our servers have nowhere to store a password.

You choose where your plan lives.

By default your tasks are in a plain-text folder on your computer and in your Knowlu account, encrypted, so any computer you sign in on shows the same day. Our servers can read that copy, because keeping your computers in step is their job. If you'd rather not keep one, choose This computer only: your plan is kept on your computer and not in your account. Knowlu still sends what it needs to work, and the policy lists all of it.

What the models see.

To estimate how long things take and what matters most, Knowlu sends a short excerpt of each task and calendar event, and of your email if you connect it, to models run by CoreWeave and DeepInfra. They read it for one request, keep nothing and train on nothing.

Email is your choice.

It is off until you connect a mailbox. Knowlu reads the start of each message, personal mail included, to tell whether it's about school, and keeps only what is. Forwarded mail is deleted as soon as Knowlu is done with it. Disconnect at any time, and the records of what Knowlu decided about your email go with it.

Staying signed in is recommended, and up to you.

Knowlu can keep you signed in to Blackboard and your mailbox on this computer, so it doesn't have to ask again. A kept sign-in is full access to that account on this computer, so keep your computer locked. It's on by default, and you can turn it off at setup or in Settings.

What we measure.

Which screens you open, what you check off, snooze or approve, and when you correct an estimate. Never your words, task titles or course names. Error logs reach us only when you send a report, and you see it first.

Who can see it.

You. No one at Knowlu reads your tasks or email without your permission, unless the law requires it. Your school doesn't see what Knowlu holds about you. The companies that run parts of Knowlu each do one job; the policy names every one.

Applying to the pilot.

Your answers on the pilot form are kept in Knowlu's Google account, used only for the pilot, and deleted when the pilot ends, or sooner if you ask.

Leaving is one button.

Settings → Delete my data removes your plan, your sign-ins and your account. We never sell or share your data.

Questions: support@knowlu.com. A person reads it.
