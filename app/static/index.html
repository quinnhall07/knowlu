      Knowlu

      Welcome

      Knowlu answers one question every morning: what should I work on today, and in what order?

      Knowlu costs $9.99 a month after a 7-day trial. You can cancel any time, in Settings.

      Your account

      Knowlu asks Google for your email address and your name, and nothing else. Your calendar and your mail are separate permissions, asked for later, and you can say no to both.

      Subscribe

      $9.99 a month, or $69.99 for the academic year. Seven days free first; your card is taken now and not charged until the trial ends. Cancel any time in Settings.

      Name this setup

      Knowlu keeps your work as plain files you can open, copy and take with you.

      Connect your calendars

      Two things, and Knowlu needs both before it can tell you what to do today: what your school says is due, and what your week already looks like.

      Your school

      Start typing its name. Every US college and university is in this list.

      Sign in the way you always do. Knowlu opens your school’s own page in a window, you sign in there, and Knowlu keeps only the calendar link — never what you type into it.

      Which does your school use?

      Or paste the link yourself: Blackboard → Calendar → Calendar Settings → Share Calendar. Canvas → Calendar → Calendar Feed.

      Your own calendar

      This is what makes today’s page know your day is already half full. In Google Calendar: Settings → click your calendar under Settings for my calendars → Integrate calendar → copy the Secret address in iCal format. Press Reset beside it first if that address has ever been shared with anyone — whoever holds it can read your calendar.

      Reads your Google Calendar so Knowlu knows when you are busy. Read-only, and you can disconnect at any time. While Knowlu is in Google's testing programme this works for invited testers and the connection needs renewing about once a week.

      Coursework logins

      Optional. Stored in Windows Credential Manager on this machine — never in the vault, never in a backup, and never sent to us.

      Which class is which?

      Knowlu found these on your accounts. Confirm the course each one belongs to — without this, Knowlu can see the work but not what it is for.

      Gmail

      Knowlu can read your Gmail for things you have to do and propose each one for you to approve. You can skip this and connect later in Settings.

      While Google reviews Knowlu, this works only for invited testers, and the connection needs renewing about once a week.
