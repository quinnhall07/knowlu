### R2-2 Important: the harness envelopes are not deterministic. Fix in text.

T5c (plan :296-302) runs `surface` with no `--today`, `--now` or `--build-sha`. `vault-full` is dated
around 2026-08-28 (its tasks are due 2026-08-26 and later). The payload's `revision` is a sha1 over the
payload (`engine/src/surface.rs:2373-2384`).

**Consequence.** Every re-run writes different envelope bytes, and every date-relative word
("Due today", overdue, the poll's repaint) changes with the calendar. T5c's "re-runnable" check means
churn in committed files, and page tasks' DOM checks pass or fail depending on the day they run.

**Fix.**
- Pass the oracle's pins, `--today 2026-08-28 --now 2026-08-28T09:00 --build-sha fixture`, and have the
  stub freeze `Date` to that `now`.
- Name the binary and its build step: `cargo build -p knowlu-engine`, then
  `target/debug/knowlu-engine.exe` in the task worktree.
- Add `console_build` and `seen_at` to the topline keys the wrapper adds (`app/src/commands.rs:34-35`).
  The plan lists five; the app adds seven.
